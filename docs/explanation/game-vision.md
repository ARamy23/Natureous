# Game Vision: Natureous

## What We Agreed On

### Story

**Setting**: A world where humans and nature coexist, centered around a cosmic entity called the World Tree.

**Timeline**:
1. Humans discover the World Tree and worship it, learning from nature and advancing their civilization through symbiosis.
2. Some humans grow arrogant, believing themselves superior to nature. They destroy nature's monuments, severing the bond.
3. Nature responds by entering a deep slumber, its energy depleted.
4. Centuries pass (medieval era) until nature awakens and releases **Mino**, a warrior born from nature's essence, tasked with reclaiming the world from humanity.
5. The game begins as Mino awakens with no memory of his power.

**Main Character (Mino)**: An avatar of nature's will. Weak at first due to the destroyed monuments, Mino grows stronger as he restores them. His ultimate goal is to end humanity's reign and restore nature's dominion.

### Game Style

**Inspiration**: Oceanhorn (Action-Adventure 2.5D).

**Tone**: Exploration-driven action with environmental storytelling. Oceanhorn's design philosophy of gradual power growth through landmark discovery informs Mino's progression.

**Visual Direction**: 2.5D isometric perspective (verified against reference art from board). Character art leverages minimalist shaders for readability (protagonist uses distinct shader vs. NPC dummies).

### Game Dimension

**Format**: 2.5D (two-and-a-half dimensions). Gameplay occurs on a 2D plane (horizontal/vertical movement) with 3D depth perception and parallax layering. Camera follows from an isometric angle.

### Combat Direction

**Decision Status**: Undecided between two approaches:
- **Beat-em-up** (methodical, rhythm-based)
- **Hack-and-slash** (preferred by design lead) — faster, more visceral, better suited to Oceanhorn's pacing

Current implementation favors hack-and-slash based on ongoing development.

### Main Character Growth

Mino awakens powerless. Progression follows an Oceanhorn model:
1. **Monument Restoration**: Seeking destroyed nature monuments
2. **Ability Unlocking**: Each monument restores or unlocks abilities (movement, combat, special powers)
3. **Power Scaling**: Both combat damage and traversal options expand
4. **Skill Tree Potential**: Ideas exist for skill points and varied attack types (melee, elemental), but remain unconfirmed

### Maps and Level Design

**Concept**: A limited number of major landmark maps, each symbolizing real-world locations. Examples include using the Hiroshima Gate model to represent Eastern culture.

**Target Scope**: Approximately 3–4 major maps sufficient for narrative arc.

**Map Transformation**: After completing a map/chapter, the environment renovates into a restored, healthier state (removing human corruption, cleaner air, flourishing nature). This visual transformation reinforces the game's environmental theme.

### Creatures and Followers

**Pet/Follower System**: A proposed feature inspired by player feedback on earlier projects. Creatures (e.g., wolves) that follow Mino and perform auxiliary actions increase engagement and provide tactical variety.

**Status**: Conceptual; no implementation prioritized yet.

## Ideas (Unprioritized Concepts)

From the board's "Ideas" list:

- **Elemental combat**: Mino bends air, water or fire with mana. Abilities unlock by finding nature monuments, hitting achievements, or buying them from a store.
- **MC growth**: new skills and move sets unlock as the player progresses.
- **Pets / followers**: e.g. wolves that follow the player and act.
- **Maps**: a few major maps, each symbolising a real-world landmark (the Hiroshima gate for eastern culture).
- **Renovated maps**: a finished map turns into clean nature with breathable air.

Planned work in the "To Do" list (cards with no further description): skill points, game coins, in-app purchases, shop, score, a backend for those, Remote Config, blocking, deflect/counter, double jump, air combo, melee weapon combos, magic attacks, puzzles, character selection, intro story scene.
